    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Work the list in five passes, in this order. Each stage names the trophies it covers and why it comes where it does — the order is built from the official descriptions: story first, then the things the story can lock away, then the open-world clean-up.

      Missable Trophies

      A trophy is marked missable when its official description ties it to a one-time story moment, a choice, or a window that closes — something you cannot go back and do after the story moves on. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the credits.

      How to read the flag

      • Story moment: tied to a specific mission or chapter; replay it from the mission menu or start the moment before it passes.
      • Choice: tied to a decision in a mission; pick the required option, or replay the mission and choose again.
      • Window: tied to a limited-time event or a state that changes as the story advances; do it while the window is open.
